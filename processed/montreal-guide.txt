Welcome to Montréal!
Montréal knows how to set itself apart. Its heritage
charm, architectural beauty, cultural vitality, economic
drive and joie de vivre make our metropolis a unique
place to explore, again and again.
As the economic engine of Québec and a major
French-speaking world metropolis, Montréal is as
much a blend of Europe and America as it is of
business and cultural experiences, or of major
festivals and neighbourhood life.
Montréal bustles with shopping, culture and sports.
And of course, the city’s many green spaces,
including Mount Royal and Parc Jean-Drapeau,
offer visitors breathtaking views of the city.
The diversity of Montréal is embodied by the
people who live here, as well as by the city’s
many neighbourhoods.
Another feature of the city is its culinary delights.
Renowned restaurants abound, with cuisines from
all over the world, from local to world-famous.
In fact, our metropolis has been listed in the
famous Michelin Guide this year.
Montréal comes alive in every season, summer
and winter alike. With flagship events like Montréal
en Lumière, Igloofest, Christmas markets and
downtown tobogganing, the city never hibernates.
I wish you an unforgettable stay with us!
Valérie Plante
Mayor of Montréal

Welcome to Montréal, dear visitors!
Whether you are here to discover our renowned festivals,
savour our unique cuisine, explore our picturesque
neighbourhoods, or simply enjoy the warm hospitality
of Montrealers, we are certain that this stay will
capture your heart.
Our metropolis is rich in history, culture, and diversity.
Stroll through Old Montréal, admire the architecture,
and let yourself be charmed by the cobblestone streets.
Explore the Plateau-Mont-Royal with its eclectic shops
and wander along Wellington Street in Verdun, named
the coolest street in the world. Enjoy green spaces like
Mount Royal Park, where you can relax and take in
a breathtaking view of the city.
We hope you fully enjoy your visit to our ever-evolving
city. Feel free to visit our Tourist Information Office for
advice and recommendations on the best ways to
enjoy your stay.

MONTRÉAL
Welcome to Montréal!
Montréal knows how to set itself apart. Its heritage
charm, architectural beauty, cultural vitality, economic
drive and joie de vivre make our metropolis a unique
place to explore, again and again.
As the economic engine of Québec and a major
French-speaking world metropolis, Montréal is as
much a blend of Europe and America as it is of
business and cultural experiences, or of major
festivals and neighbourhood life.
Montréal bustles with shopping, culture and sports.
And of course, the city’s many green spaces,
including Mount Royal and Parc Jean-Drapeau,
offer visitors breathtaking views of the city.
The diversity of Montréal is embodied by the
people who live here, as well as by the city’s
many neighbourhoods.
Another feature of the city is its culinary delights.
Renowned restaurants abound, with cuisines from
all over the world, from local to world-famous.
In fact, our metropolis has been listed in the
famous Michelin Guide this year.
Montréal comes alive in every season, summer
and winter alike. With flagship events like Montréal
en Lumière, Igloofest, Christmas markets and
downtown tobogganing, the city never hibernates.
I wish you an unforgettable stay with us!
Valérie Plante
Mayor of Montréal
