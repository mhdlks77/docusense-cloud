Welcome to Montréal—a city known for its rich heritage, architectural beauty, cultural vibrancy, economic vitality, and welcoming spirit. As Québec’s economic engine and a major French-speaking metropolis, Montréal uniquely blends European and North American influences, offering visitors a dynamic mix of business, festivals, neighborhood life, and diverse experiences.

Key highlights:

- **Diversity & Culture:** Montréal is home to people from over 120 nationalities, contributing to its cosmopolitan character. Its neighborhoods each offer a distinct atmosphere, from historic Old Montréal to trendy Plateau-Mont-Royal and dynamic Verdun.
- **Year-Round Activities:** The city offers green spaces like Mount Royal and Parc Jean-Drapeau, iconic events (Montréal en Lumière, Igloofest, Christmas markets), renowned museums (Montreal Museum of Fine Arts), and the lively Casino de Montréal.
- **Gastronomy:** Montréal’s culinary scene is renowned internationally, with hundreds of restaurants representing global cuisines. The city is recognized in the Michelin Guide.
- **History & Heritage:** Montréal’s history is rooted in its Indigenous origins and centuries of multicultural migration, from its First Nations roots (notably the Kanien’kehá:ka, or Mohawk people) to waves of French and British settlers and newcomers from around the globe.
- **Practical Info & Accessibility:** The guide features sections on transportation, neighborhoods, events, and things to do, making it easy for visitors to plan their trip and experience Montréal like a local.

In short, whether you’re interested in arts, food, festivals, shopping, or simply exploring vibrant neighborhoods, Montréal offers something for everyone, in every season—a warm and unforgettable destination.
